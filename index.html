Hi, I'm Szen.

I currently live in Ashburn, Virginia in the US. I'm working as a Postdoctoral Scientist in the labs of Dr. Alejandro Aguilera-Castrejon (HHMI's Janelia Research Campus) and Sanjay Srivatsan (Fred Hutch Cancer Centre). During my PhD, I was supervised by Dr. Mathew Garnett and Dr. Sam Behjati at the Wellcome Sanger Institute in Hinxton in the UK. Prior to my PhD, I completed medical school at the University of Sheffield and practiced medicine in the UK's National Health Service.

Key research areas of interest I'm actively involved in:

  • Clonal selection, cellular plasticity, and memory. Cellular plasticity and mechanisms of cell fate decisions during early mammalian development.
  • Single-cell profiling technologies. Development of highly multiplexed, single-cell co-assays.
  • Perturbation data analysis and modeling. Computational strategies to analyse scRNA-seq perturbation datasets.

In my PhD, I identified regulators of cellular plasticity and treatment adaptation in KRAS-mutant colorectal cancer by applying combinatorial indexing technology to profile colorectal cancer organoids following targeted therapy at single-cell resolution. I also co-developed an atlas of colorectal cancer and linked to perturbation data to understand perturbations that drive cells toward defined phenotypic extremes.
